# Trading Learnings & Accumulated Wisdom

## Core Principles Discovered

### Market Structure
- Zero Nifty 500 stocks at 52-week lows = strong bullish regime. All action on breakout side.
- When FII selling slows + DII buying continues = "buy on dips" regime confirmed.
- Softening crude is India's single biggest macro tailwind (import bill, inflation, RBI room).

### Sector Rotation
- Money rotates, it doesn't disappear. Exit laggards fast, enter leaders faster.
- **No sector leads forever.** Pharma leads this week, IT or metals might lead next week. Always research fresh — never carry last week's bias into this week's trades.
- **Every Sunday: actively research what's ABOUT to rotate, not just what already rotated.** Use online sources (ET Markets, Moneycontrol, Livemint, Trendlyne) to check sectoral index performance, FII sector flows, and upcoming catalysts.
- Sector leadership drivers change: RBI rate cuts → banks/NBFC/real estate. Weak rupee → IT/pharma exporters. Crude fall → airlines/paints/OMCs. China stimulus → metals. Government capex push → infra/defence/railways. Monsoon deficit → avoid FMCG/agri.
- PSU bank cycle (2024-2026) is maturing — profit-booking visible. Private banks in "recovery re-rating" phase.
- Pharma (Jul 2026): Structural winner due to rupee weakness + FDA pipeline clearance + GLP-1/biosimilar demand. But this could fade — always verify with live data.
- Capital goods/power T&D: After 100-300% runs, profit-booking is violent. Don't hold into distribution.
- **Rule: If a sector led for 3+ consecutive weeks, expect profit-booking. The crowd is already in. Look for the NEXT rotation, not the current one.**

### Stock Selection
- Best swing candidates: 52-wk high + volume expansion + delivery > 50% + sector tailwind.
- P/E alone means nothing. ROCE + OPM trend + revenue growth trajectory = quality filter.
- Lupin-type setups (13% → 33% OPM expansion) = operating leverage plays. These are the real compounders.
- Avoid "cheapest in sector" traps (Aurobindo). Cheap + flat margins = value trap dressed as value.
- Small-caps at 85x P/E (Acutaas) = better as long-term holds. Don't swing-trade them near highs.

### Position Sizing
- 30-stock portfolio at ₹5.5L = over-diversification theatre. Aim for 12-15 meaningful positions.
- Sub-1% positions are dead weight. Either size up or exit.
- For swing trading: max 4-5 positions, ₹40K each, total ₹1.2-1.5L deployed.
- Ticket size matters: ₹12,500/share stocks (Bajaj Finance) don't work for small swing capital.

### Risk Management
- Stop-loss is sacred. The one time you don't use it is the one time it costs 15%.
- Time-stop is underrated. Flat trades for 4 days = capital doing nothing. Exit and redeploy.
- Never hold through earnings for a swing trade. That's gambling, not trading.
- Market kill-switch (Nifty below 50 EMA) saves you from the big drawdowns.

## Patterns That Work

1. **52-Week High Breakout + Sector Tailwind:** Highest probability swing setup.
2. **Multi-Month Base Breakout (IndusInd type):** Re-rating plays after structural problems resolve.
3. **OPM Expansion + Revenue Growth:** Operating leverage = profit grows faster than revenue.
4. **Fresh institutional accumulation:** High delivery % + price up = smart money entering.

## Patterns That Fail

1. **Chasing gap-ups without pullback:** FOMO entries have the worst R:R.
2. **"Cheap" stocks in downtrends:** Cheap can get cheaper (CANBK vs BANKBARODA in same sector).
3. **Solo breakouts against sector trend:** Stock going up while sector going down = fake breakout risk.
4. **Averaging down on losers:** Throwing good money after bad. Cut fast.
