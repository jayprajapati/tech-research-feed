### Top 3 Macro Catalysts

1. **Middle East De-escalation:** Easing geopolitical tensions (US-Iran situation calming) removed the crude oil risk premium. Brent softened, providing direct relief to India's import bill, inflation trajectory, and overall risk appetite. This was the single biggest sentiment driver of the week.

2. **US Labor Market Softening → Fed Rate Hike Expectations Collapse:** Softer US jobs data significantly reduced expectations of further Fed rate hikes. Dollar weakened on the week, rupee stabilized, and FII selling pressure eased. Markets began pricing in a "higher for longer but not higher still" Fed stance.

3. **India-Japan Summit & Domestic Tailwinds:** Positive outcomes from the India-Japan bilateral summit boosted sentiment around defence, infrastructure, and semiconductor cooperation. Combined with softening crude and upcoming Q1FY27 earnings season, the domestic narrative remained constructive.

**FII/DII Flow Context:** FII selling slowed meaningfully during the week as crude cooled and global risk appetite improved. DIIs continued providing support on dips. The "buy on dips" regime remains intact.

- **Direct News Trigger:** Company issued a **weak revenue guidance outlook** for upcoming quarters, signaling deceleration in the auto-tech/EV engineering services vertical. Management commentary on delayed client programs and margin pressure triggered a massive institutional exit. Multiple brokerages downgraded the stock post-guidance.

### Bharti Airtel & Bajaj Finance — Weekly: Strong outperformers (top mcap gainers)
- **Technical Change:** Both stocks contributed the most to the Rs 1 lakh crore mcap surge among top-10 valued firms. Airtel breaking into new all-time high territory.
- **Direct News Trigger:** Airtel benefiting from ARPU upgrade cycle expectations and 5G monetization. Bajaj Finance riding the consumer credit expansion theme with strong AUM growth data. Both are FII-heavy names that rallied as foreign selling pressure eased.

### HFCL — Friday: +4.84%
- **Technical Change:** Broke out of a multi-week consolidation range on expanding volumes.
- **Direct News Trigger:** Defence/telecom order book momentum. The stock is benefiting from the India-Japan Summit announcements around digital infrastructure and 6G cooperation, plus ongoing BSNL 4G order execution.

### BHEL — Friday: -4.57%
- **Technical Change:** Broke below its 20-day EMA, showing distribution after a massive multi-year rally. Bearish engulfing on daily chart.
- **Direct News Trigger:** Profit-booking in the entire capital goods/power equipment basket. The sector had been overbought for weeks. Institutional rotation out of capex plays into pharma/IT as the "new quality" trade for Q2.

### Policy Bazaar (PB Fintech) — Most active stock by turnover (Rs 3,694 crore)
- **Technical Change:** Massive volume surge indicating institutional repositioning.
- **Direct News Trigger:** Ahead of Q1 results, expectations of profitability inflection and insurance distribution growth. High-conviction institutional buying visible in delivery data.

- RSI has registered a **bullish crossover** — momentum confirming price
- Smallcap index "better placed than Nifty" per technical analysts — breadth expanding

### Sentiment Tone: **"Buy on Dips towards 23,800-24,000"**

Justification: With crude softening, Fed hike fears fading, and Q1FY27 earnings season providing stock-specific catalysts, the path of least resistance is higher. However, with Nifty approaching 24,500 resistance, expect intermittent profit-booking. The strategy is to accumulate on any dip towards the 24,000-23,800 demand zone rather than chase at highs.

### Key Monitorables for Next Week:
1. **Q1FY27 Earnings** — First batch of results (IT majors, banks). Management commentary on demand outlook will set sector-level direction.
2. **US-India FTA Progress** — Any concrete trade deal announcements will be a significant positive catalyst.
3. **Monsoon Deficit** — Widening monsoon deficit could pressure FMCG/agri names and raise food inflation concerns.
4. **Crude Oil Trajectory** — If Brent sustains below $72, it's a structural positive for Indian equities. Any flare-up in Middle East = immediate risk-off.
5. **FII Flow Data** — Watch if the slowdown in selling converts to net buying. That would be the trigger for Nifty to punch through 24,500-24,800.

## Summary

The week was characterized by a **broad-based risk-on rally** driven by improving global macro (cooling geopolitics, softer Fed stance) and strong domestic tailwinds (crude relief, bilateral diplomacy). The market is transitioning from a "cautious recovery" to a "confirmed uptrend" technically. Key rotation: **OUT of** capital goods/PSU banks (profit-booking after multi-year runs) → **INTO** pharma (structural), IT (tactical recovery), and financials (FII-favoured).

The portfolio implications are clear: trim extended winners in capex/PSU bank themes, and position for the next leg in pharma, quality financials, and select metals with China stimulus exposure.
